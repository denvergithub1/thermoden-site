ThermoGen Biotech

Caribbean hot springs host microbial communities that have evolved under extreme heat, acidity, and sulfur pressure for thousands of years, producing biology found nowhere else. These environments remain almost entirely unstudied. ThermoGen is the first to systematically access and translate this biology into commercial products.

What we do

Three verticals, one source environment

Each grows from the same proprietary microbial collection.

Novel Plasmid DNA

Extremophile organisms carry plasmids encoding heat tolerance, acid resistance, and metal detoxification, traits absent in conventional lab strains. We are developing a proprietary catalogue for biopharma and gene therapy applications.

Biopharma · Gene Therapy

Antibiotic Discovery

Microbes competing in extreme environments produce antimicrobial compounds to survive. We screen novel isolates for activity against drug resistant pathogens on WHO priority lists.

AMR · Drug Discovery

Environmental Applications

Microbial communities from sulfur rich, metal laden spring environments show capabilities relevant to bioremediation and sustainable industrial processes. Details available under NDA.
